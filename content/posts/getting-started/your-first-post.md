## Frontmatter

Every post starts with YAML metadata:

```md
---
title: Your first post
description: A one-line summary for cards and SEO.
date: 2026-11-03
tags: [getting-started]
---
```

## Body

Write in Markdown. Use headings, lists, code blocks, and images from `public/` or absolute URLs.

When you are ready, commit on a branch, open a PR, and merge to `main` so your host can rebuild the site.
