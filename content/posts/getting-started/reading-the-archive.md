The archive is meant to be browsed, not scrolled endlessly without a plan.

## Categories

Each category is a topic folder. Open **Networking** for DNS and HTTP, **Security** for hashing and related notes, **JavaScript** for language patterns, and so on.

## Tags

Tags cut across categories. If you care about `tls`, `docker`, or `sql`, open the tag page and read every matching post in one place.

## Featured and latest

Featured posts are curated starting points. The latest post card on the home page is simply the most recently published article.

## Related posts

At the bottom of an article you will usually see other posts from the same category. Use those when you want the next step on the same topic.
