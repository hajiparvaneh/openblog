OpenBlog is a free blog you can run yourself. It is built for people who want a simple public site without a heavy CMS, a paid host, or a locked-in editor.

## What you get

- A readable home page with featured posts and an archive
- Categories and tags so readers can browse by topic
- An About page you can edit in plain Markdown

## Who it is for

Writers, developers, students, and anyone who wants a personal or project blog that stays under their control.

Start with the featured posts, open the **Blog** category, or follow tags like `openblog` and `markdown` to learn how the site works.
