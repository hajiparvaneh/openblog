OpenBlog is **free** and **open source**. The full template lives on GitHub so you can read the code, copy the project, and change it to match your brand.

## MIT license

The project uses the **MIT** license. That means you can use it for personal or commercial blogs, modify the layouts, and redistribute your own version, as long as you keep the license notice where the license requires it.

## What “open” means here

- The source is public
- Posts are plain Markdown files you own
- Publishing can run on GitHub Pages with no paid blogging platform in the middle

If you want your own site, clone or use the repository as a template, replace the sample posts, update the About page, and publish. The three posts in this starter archive explain the idea; the rest of the archive is yours to fill.
