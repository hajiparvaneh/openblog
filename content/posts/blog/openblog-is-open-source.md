Most projects say they are open. OpenBlog keeps the whole blogging kit in a public repository you can read end to end.

## What is public

- Site source and layouts
- Sample posts and documentation pages
- GitHub Pages workflow
- MIT license

You can inspect how posts load, how categories and tags are built, and how the static site is generated.

## What you customize

- Branding and copy in `openblog.config.ts`
- Logos and icons in `public/`
- About page in `content/about.md`
- Posts in `content/posts/`

## What stays simple

This template does not require OAuth, a Worker, scoring, or a leaderboard to run. Clone it, write Markdown, and publish.

If you want a live community preview with extra demo features, see [openblog.cc](https://openblog.cc) and its separate preview repository.
